Put Little One on your Home Screen

It works like a normal app from there, keeps your entries safely on this phone and works offline.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Little One from your Home Screen.
Use the Home Screen app rather than this Safari tab. iPhones keep their storage separate, so entries made here won't appear there.
